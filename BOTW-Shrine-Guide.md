# The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild — Complete Shrine Guide

**All 136 shrines** (120 base game + 16 *The Champions' Ballad* DLC) with exact in-game map
coordinates and full step-by-step walkthroughs.

### How to read the coordinates

Coordinates are given in Sheikah Slate order — **(X, Y, Z)** where:

- **X** — east (+) / west (−)
- **Y** — north (+) / south (−)
- **Z** — elevation above sea level

Values are pulled straight from the game's own map data files, so they match what the
Sheikah Slate shows when you hover the map cursor over the shrine.

# Full walkthroughs

## Great Plateau Region — 4 shrines

### Ja Baij Shrine — *Bomb Trial*

**Coordinates:** `(-0448, -1990, 0193)`  
**Location:** East of the Shrine of Resurrection  
**Rewards:** Remote Bomb Rune, Traveler's Claymore, Amber, Spirit Orb

**Getting there.** This shrine will be available after activating the Great Plateau Tower at the beginning of the game.

**Walkthrough**

1. Interact with the nearby pedestal to obtain the Remote Bomb Rune.
2. Use any of the two Remote Bombs that you obtained to break the wall ahead. Press the L button to detonate the remote bomb after dropping it.
3. Break the wall on your left and climb the ladder. Before proceeding to the main area of the shrine, break the wall on the right-side corridor for a chest containing a Traveler's Claymore.
4. Place a cube-shaped Remote Bomb on the moving platform and detonate it once it gets near the breakable wall.
5. Proceed to the next area and ascend the stairs to your right. Get on the left moving block on the other side and let it launch you to the other side for a chest containing an Amber.
6. Place a spherical Remote Bomb into the tube to launch it at the breakable wall. Detonate the bomb once it hits the wall.
7. Climb the ladder to the other side of the area and examine the altar for a Spirit Orb.

**Treasure chests**

- *Treasure Chest #1* — Ja Baij Shrine Treasure Chest #1 Location 1 Once you get the Remote Bomb Rune and break the first wall at the entrance, go right at the fork and bomb another wall for a chest containing a Traveler's Claymore.
- *Treasure Chest #2* — Ja Baij Shrine Treasure Chest #2 Location 1 On the left side of the altar room, get on the farthest launch block and let it send you to the other side for a chest containing an Amber.


### Keh Namut Shrine — *Cryonis Trial*

**Coordinates:** `(-1435, -1991, 0351)`  
**Location:** On the snowy northwest shoulder of Mount Hylia  
**Rewards:** Cryonis Rune, Traveler's Spear, Spirit Orb

**Getting there.** This shrine will be available after activating the Great Plateau Tower at the beginning of the game.

**Walkthrough**

1. Interact with the nearby pedestal to obtain the Cryonis Rune.
2. Create an ice pillar with Cryonis to reach the elevated platform ahead.
3. In the next area, raise the gate by creating an ice pillar beneath it before moving on.
4. Defeat the Guardian Scout and continue to the other side. Aim your arrow at its eye to stun it. You can find a chest in this area containing a Traveler's Spear that you can reach using Cryonis.
5. Create an ice pillar on either side of the platform and use it to get to the area above.
6. Examine the altar and you will obtain a Spirit Orb.

**Tips**

- *Use the Ice Pillar to Block Attacks* — You can create an ice pillar to block beams from the Guardian Scout in this area. You can also use the same pillar to get near the Guardian Scout before attacking it.


### Oman Au Shrine — *Magnesis Trial*

**Coordinates:** `(-0672, -1514, 0186)`  
**Location:** Northwest of the Great Plateau Tower  
**Rewards:** Magnesis Rune, Spirit Orb, Traveler's Bow

**Getting there.** This shrine will be available after activating the Great Plateau Tower at the beginning of the game.

**Walkthrough**

1. Upon entering, examine the pedestal to obtain the Magnesis Rune.
2. Use the Magnesis Rune on the metal plates on the floor to reveal an opening then proceed to the other side.
3. Use the same rune on the metal block on the wall and take down the other blocks with it. You can press up on the D-pad to push. Be careful not to get hit by the falling blocks!
4. Defeat the Guardian Scout then get across to the second platform. Use the metal plate that you used as a bridge to get to the last platform.
5. Open the gate with Magnesis and examine the altar to obtain a Spirit Orb. Look on your left before going through the gate for a chest containing a Traveler's Bow.

**Tips**

- *Use Metal Objects Against Enemies* — The metal objects that you can grab with Magnesis can be used to attack enemies to save your weapon from wearing down. Take note that you will also take damage if the object hits you so be careful with this rune!


### Owa Daim Shrine — *Stasis Trial*

**Coordinates:** `(-0925, -2320, 0287)`  
**Location:** Southwest of the Temple of Time, on the slope of Mount Hylia  
**Rewards:** Stasis Rune, Traveler's Shield, Iron Sledgehammer, Spirit Orb, -

**Getting there.** This shrine will be available after activating the Great Plateau Tower at the beginning of the game.

**Walkthrough**

1. Interact with the nearby pedestal to obtain the Stasis Rune.
2. Use the rune on the giant gear to stop its movement and use the bridge to get across.
3. Moving on, wait for a boulder to fall and when the path is clear, quickly run to the next area. Use the Stasis rune on the incoming boulder to prevent it from blocking your path.
4. Go left and pick up the Iron Sledgehammer by the wall. You will need this to move the boulder blocking the next path. Continue walking to where the boulders come from for a chest containing a Traveler's Shield.
5. Use the Stasis Rune on the boulder ahead and hit it with your Iron Sledgehammer. Wait for the time freeze to wear off and it will be launched out of your way.
6. Examine the altar and you will obtain a Spirit Orb.

**Tips**

- *Use Stasis Rune to Move Large Objects* — Objects frozen with the Stasis Rune store the kinetic energy they receive and release it all at once when the freeze effect wear off. An arrow will indicate the distance and direction it will launch to after the time freeze which depends on the amount of energy stored and where you hit the object.

## Dueling Peaks Region — 9 shrines

### Bosh Kala Shrine — *The Wind Guides You*

**Coordinates:** `(0087, -1658, 0136)`  
**Location:** Proxim Bridge, West of Dueling Peaks Tower  
**Rewards:** Amber, Soldier's Claymore, Spirit Orb

**Getting there.** Bosh Kala Shrine can be found near Proxim Bridge in the Dueling Peaks region. You can get to the shrine faster by fast travelling to the Dueling Peaks Tower and paragliding west until you reach Proxim Bridge.

**Walkthrough**

1. Move to the next room and ride the gust of wind on your left with your paraglider to get to the other side.
2. Ride another gust of wind to reach the platform at the end of the room. Be sure to drop once you're above the platform.
3. Examine the altar to receive a Spirit Orb.

**Treasure chests**

- *Treasure Chest #1* — Near the entrance of the shrine is a fan that blows a strong wind. Ride this wind to reach the chest containing an Amber.
- *Treasure Chest #2* — Ride the gust of wind that blows towards the altar and just before you reach the platform to the altar, steer right toward the ledge with a chest. This may take a few tries so know the perfect timing! Open the chest to receive a Soldier's Claymore.


### Ha Dahamar Shrine — *The Water Guides*

**Coordinates:** `(1663, -1922, 0129)`

- *Perfect Dodge Backflip* — Flurry rush also works on doing perfect dodge backflips, which is more effective if your enemy attacks horizontally.
- *Performing a Perfect Guard* — Using the same timing precision with perfect dodge, you can parry an enemy's attack by pressing A right before an attack hits. How to Parry (Perfect Guard)
- *Charging an Attack* — Holding the attack button will cause your weapon to charge up. Once it starts consuming stamina, you can release it for a powerful attack.

### Kihiro Moh Shrine — *Inside the Box*

**Coordinates:** `(-3392, -1347, 0451)`  
**Location:** Near the Yiga Clan Hideout  
**Rewards:** Thunderspear, Naboris's Emblem

**Getting there.** You can find the shrine within the Yiga Clan Hideout. To access the shrine, you need to clear all four Divine Beasts and start EX Champion Urbosa's Song quest. EX Champion Urbosa's Song Walkthrough

**Walkthrough**

1. Head up the stairs and inspect the motion controls apparatus. Move the box and count how many orbs there are inside and what colors there are. The box contains one red orb, two green orbs, two yellow orbs, and four purple orbs.
2. Flip the box so that the side with the eye is facing upwards. This step is vital for getting the treasure later.
3. Place the correct amount of orbs on the receptacles. The large glowing orbs on the wall will serve as a guide where to place them. The receptacle closest to the wall is 1, while the second last receptacle from the wall is 4.
4. Head inside and inspect the shrine to receive Naboris's Emblem.
