Ja Baij Shrine — Bomb Trial
East of the Shrine of Resurrection
-0448, -1990, 0193

Getting there

This shrine will be available after activating the Great Plateau Tower at the beginning of the game.

Rewards

Remote Bomb RuneTraveler's ClaymoreAmberSpirit Orb

Walkthrough

  1. Interact with the nearby pedestal to obtain the Remote Bomb Rune.
  2. Use any of the two Remote Bombs that you obtained to break the wall ahead. Press the L button to detonate the remote bomb after dropping it.
  3. Break the wall on your left and climb the ladder. Before proceeding to the main area of the shrine, break the wall on the right-side corridor for a chest containing a Traveler's Claymore.
  4. Place a cube-shaped Remote Bomb on the moving platform and detonate it once it gets near the breakable wall.
  5. Proceed to the next area and ascend the stairs to your right. Get on the left moving block on the other side and let it launch you to the other side for a chest containing an Amber.
  6. Place a spherical Remote Bomb into the tube to launch it at the breakable wall. Detonate the bomb once it hits the wall.
  7. Climb the ladder to the other side of the area and examine the altar for a Spirit Orb.

Treasure chests

  • Treasure Chest #1 — Ja Baij Shrine Treasure Chest #1 Location 1 Once you get the Remote Bomb Rune and break the first wall at the entrance, go right at the fork and bomb another wall for a chest containing a Traveler's Claymore.
  • Treasure Chest #2 — Ja Baij Shrine Treasure Chest #2 Location 1 On the left side of the altar room, get on the farthest launch block and let it send you to the other side for a chest containing an Amber.

Walkthrough

Owa Daim Shrine — Stasis Trial
Southwest of the Temple of Time, on the slope of Mount Hylia
-0925, -2320, 0287

Getting there

This shrine will be available after activating the Great Plateau Tower at the beginning of the game.

Rewards

Stasis RuneTraveler's ShieldIron SledgehammerSpirit Orb-

Walkthrough

  1. Interact with the nearby pedestal to obtain the Stasis Rune.
  2. Use the rune on the giant gear to stop its movement and use the bridge to get across.
  3. Moving on, wait for a boulder to fall and when the path is clear, quickly run to the next area. Use the Stasis rune on the incoming boulder to prevent it from blocking your path.
  4. Go left and pick up the Iron Sledgehammer by the wall. You will need this to move the boulder blocking the next path. Continue walking to where the boulders come from for a chest containing a Traveler's Shield.
  5. Use the Stasis Rune on the boulder ahead and hit it with your Iron Sledgehammer. Wait for the time freeze to wear off and it will be launched out of your way.
  6. Examine the altar and you will obtain a Spirit Orb.

Tips

  • Use Stasis Rune to Move Large Objects — Objects frozen with the Stasis Rune store the kinetic energy they receive and release it all at once when the freeze effect wear off. An arrow will indicate the distance and direction it will launch to after the time freeze which depends on the amount of energy stored and where you hit the object.

Ta'loh Naeg Shrine — Ta'loh Naeg's Teaching
This shrine can be found at the north of Kakariko Village.
1841, -0891, 0273

Rewards

Eightfold BladeShield of the Mind's EyeOpalSpirit Orb

Walkthrough

  1. Walk to the center to start your training against a damaged Guardian Scout II. Treasures: Open the chest on your left for an Eightfold Blade and the chest on your right for a Shield of the Mind's Eye.
  2. Peform a perfect dodge sideways and attack the guardian with flurry rush.
  3. Peform a perfect dodge with a backflip and attack the guardian with flurry rush.
  4. Perform a perfect guard against the guardian.
  5. Build up your attack and release it once it starts charging with your stamina to finish off the guardian.
  6. Proceed to the altar room and examine the altar to receive a Spirit Orb. Treasure: Open the chest for an Opal.

Treasure chests

  • Treasure Chest #1 — #1 1 Before the sparring, you can open a chest on the left side of the room for an Eightfold Blade.
  • Treasure Chest #2 — #2 1 The chest on the right side of the room contains a Shield of the Mind's Eye.
  • Treasure Chest #3 — #3 1 Upon entering the room, you will see a chest containing an Opal.

Tips

  • Performing a Perfect Dodge — You can perform a perfect dodge by dodging at the last second before an attack hits you. If done correctly, a slowdown will occur where you can execute flurry rush as a counterattack by pressing Y. How to Perfect Dodge (Flurry Rush)
  • Perfect Dodge Backflip — Flurry rush also works on doing perfect dodge backflips, which is more effective if your enemy attacks horizontally.
  • Performing a Perfect Guard — Using the same timing precision with perfect dodge, you can parry an enemy's attack by pressing A right before an attack hits. How to Parry (Perfect Guard)
  • Charging an Attack — Holding the attack button will cause your weapon to charge up. Once it starts consuming stamina, you can release it for a powerful attack.

This shrine is found on Tenoko Island southeast of Ebon Mountain. Use the cliff southwest of this island and glide towards it.

  1. Proceed to the arena where you will be pitted against a Guardian Scout IV.
  2. Defeating this guardian will reward you with its weapons along with Ancient materials and open the gate to the altar room. Treasure: Open the chest in the altar room for a Climbing Gear.

Treasure chests

  • Treasure Chest #1 — Chaas Qeta Shrine Treasure Chest #1 1 This chest is right in front of the altar. Defeat the guardian in this shrine to access the altar room along with this chest containing a Climbing Gear.

Tips

  • Use the Pillars to Block Its Spinning Attack — One of the guardian's first attacks that you should look out for is its spinning attack. It will jump back and from its position, it will start spinning and charge at you. Use the pillars to block the attack before letting in a few hits while the guardian is staggered.
  • Perfect Parry Its Spinning Attack — Alternatively, you can parry its spinning attack. It takes some practice though and blocking its attack can quickly lower the durability of your shield. It's best to use a Guardian-type Shield against it as it has higher resistance to attacks from Guardians. How to Parry (Perfect Guard)
  • Glide During the Spinning Laser Attack — After the guardian's health drops to about 50%, it will start spinning around while firing a laser. This will create a strong upward wind that you can glide on before approaching the guardian and landing an attack on it.
  • Watch Out for Its Last Resort — Once you have whittled the guardian's health down to critical level, it will start charging for several consecutive beams. Dodge these beams while trying to get close to the guardian then quickly attack it while it's recharging. Get back when it's close to firing more beams. Repeat this process until it is defeated. You can also try perfect parrying one of the beams to end its suffering though you should also be careful as it can kill you instead if you failed to parry it.
